The AAA-Bench annotations in this folder (questions, gold actions, decision bases, pairings, evidence
fields and clarifications), the human-study files in `human_study/`, the saved model responses in
`model_outputs/` and the pre-study item pool in `construction/` are released under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The images are not part of this release. Items refer to images of the COCO 2017 dataset by file
name, and you download them from COCO yourself. The COCO annotations are licensed under CC BY 4.0
by the COCO Consortium, and each image keeps the licence of its original Flickr upload; see the
[COCO terms of use](https://cocodataset.org/#termsofuse).

The responses in `model_outputs/` were produced by models released under their own licences:
LLaVA-1.5, LLaVA-NeXT, Qwen2-VL, Qwen2.5-VL, InternVL3, Phi-3.5-Vision and Idefics3.
